Common questions

When you place an order, a personal shopper picks and packs your items at the store. Choose delivery to get your order brought to your door, or curbside pickup to collect it at the store at a time that works for you.

Delivery starts at $3.99 for same-day orders over $35. Fees vary for one-hour deliveries, club store deliveries, and deliveries under $35. Pickup orders have no delivery fee, and there is no membership required.

Some retailers offer everyday store prices, while others may set prices slightly higher than in store to help cover the costs of picking and delivery. You can always see an item's price before you add it to your cart.

You can add preferences to each item — pick a preferred replacement or ask for a refund if it's out of stock — and chat with your shopper while they shop to give specific instructions.

Instacart's 100% quality guarantee has you covered. If something's wrong — a missing item, a poor replacement, or a late order — report it within 7 days and we'll make it right with a refund or credit.
